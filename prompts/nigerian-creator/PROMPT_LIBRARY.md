# Nigerian Creator Prompt Library

**Gen Z TikTok · Instagram Reels · WhatsApp · Telegram — prompts that drive clicks, bookings, orders and money.**

Copy-and-paste prompts for a Nigerian digital entrepreneur / content creator who sells products and services online (event business, food business, skincare, digital hustles) and closes sales on WhatsApp. Every prompt works as-is in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any similar AI.

---

## How to use (60 seconds)

1. Open a new chat. Paste **Prompt 0.1 (Master Context)** first and fill the `[brackets]`.
2. Paste any prompt below. Replace the remaining `[brackets]` with your real product, ₦ price, city, audience.
3. Output sounds foreign or corporate? Paste **Prompt 0.2 (Naija Context Pack)** or reply: *"Make it more Naija Gen Z, shorter, less formal."*
4. Output too Pidgin-heavy? Reply: *"Less Pidgin, simple English, keep the vibe."*
5. Save what converts. Re-run weekly with your new numbers.

**Placeholders:** `[PRODUCT]` `[PRICE]` `[CITY]` `[AUDIENCE]` `[CTA]` `[GOAL]` `[PROOF]` — always fill them. The more specific you are, the better the output.

### Prompt stacks (ready workflows)

| Goal | Run these in order |
|---|---|
| A full week of content in 1 hour | 0.1 → 1.2 → 2.5 → 3.1 → 4.1 |
| Launch a new product or offer | 0.1 → 7.1 → 1.3 → 4.3 → 5.2 → 8.5 |
| Content flopping, sales low | 0.1 → 1.5 → 8.3 → 9.2 |

| Grow Telegram from TikTok | 0.1 → 5.1 → 3.2 → 8.4 |
| New account, zero trust yet | 0.1 → 8.7 → 7.5 → 2.1 |

```
Where I post: TikTok, Instagram Reels, WhatsApp Status, Telegram channel.
Where I sell: WhatsApp DM → [bank transfer / Opay / Paystack link / pay on delivery in Lagos]

Goal this month: [e.g. 100 WhatsApp clicks/day, 30 orders/week, 15 event bookings, 500 Telegram members]

Rules: no fake claims, no fake urgency, no bleaching/whitening claims for skincare, no income promises, every piece of content ends with ONE clear next step (DM a word, WhatsApp link in bio, comment, save, share).

Reply "Got it" and wait for my next instruction.
```

### 0.2 — NAIJA GEN Z CONTEXT PACK (paste when output sounds foreign)

```
Use this Nigerian Gen Z context in everything you write for me from now on:
- Language: simple English + light Pidgin seasoning, never full Pidgin. Words that fit: abeg, oya, sha, abi, omo, e choke, sapa, soft life, japa, cruise, no wahala, sharp sharp, ginger, jaiye, chop life, God abeg, God when, on God, dey play, e go be. Use 1–2 per piece and never force them.
- Money: prices in ₦. Payment = bank transfer, Opay/Palmpay/Moniepoint, Paystack/Flutterwave link, pay on delivery (Lagos). Payday is the 25th–30th. Buyers compare prices and fear scams, so show proof and be clear on delivery and refunds.
- Delivery: dispatch rider (same-day in Lagos, Island vs Mainland fees), GIG/waybill for other states, pickup option.
- Real-life references: NEPA/light wahala, Lagos traffic, danfo/Bolt, data/sub cost, rent, 9-5 stress, school/exams, NYSC, japa plans, small-business-owner life, owambe/aso ebi, small chops, jollof.
- Calendar hooks: payday, Detty December, Valentine's, Easter, Sallah/Eid, Children's Day (May 27), June 12, Independence Day (Oct 1), back-to-school (Sept), Black Friday, birthdays, wedding season (Nov–Jan, Easter).
- Funnel: TikTok + Reels bring attention → WhatsApp closes the sale → Telegram and WhatsApp Status keep customers coming back.
- Don'ts: no corporate words ("esteemed customer", "we are pleased to"), no fake urgency, no fake results, no bleaching/whitening promises, no income promises.
Confirm with "Naija mode on" and continue.
```
