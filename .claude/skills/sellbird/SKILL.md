# Sellbird sales playbooks

Source: https://github.com/ethanplusai/sellbird (commit b212385), `skills/` directory, AGPL-3.0 (see LICENSE, NOTICE).
These are knowledge files; the Sellbird app itself (Python pipeline, dashboard, mail sending) is not installed.

Read only the reference(s) the task needs:

| Task | Read |
|---|---|
| Write / review a cold email or sequence, subject lines | `references/email_frameworks.md` (compliance section always wins) |
| Opening line from a buying signal (hiring, funding, tech stack) | `references/signal_playbook.md` |
| Find leads without paid tools | `references/prospecting_tactics.md` |
| Qualify / score a lead | `references/lead_qualification.md` |
| Several contacts at one company, entry point, multi-threading | `references/account_navigation.md` |
| Reply to a prospect, conversation flow, tone | `references/sales_methodology.md` |
| Objections ("too expensive", "not now", competitor) | `references/objection_handling.md` |
| When/how to make an offer, closing | `references/offer_strategy.md` |
| LinkedIn connection/messaging | `references/linkedin_outreach.md` (read its risk & ethics section) |
| Prospect is YC/accelerator-backed or recently funded | `references/selling_to_startups.md` |

Notes:
- The playbooks are written for Sellbird's agents ("Scout", "Writer", "Handler"); apply the guidance directly, ignore references to its internal code.
- They are English-market oriented. When writing in Russian or for the Russian market, keep the principles (short, specific, honest, one clear ask) and adapt phrasing and legal rules (e.g. 152-ФЗ, 38-ФЗ «О рекламе») instead of translating literally.
- Never send anything on the user's behalf without explicit approval; drafts only by default.
